# Link Cell Therapies

> Clinical-stage cell therapy company in South San Francisco developing AND-gate logic-gated CAR T
> cell therapies for solid tumors and certain hematologic cancers. Founded by Stanford investigators
> Robbie Majzner and Crystal Mackall in partnership with Samsara BioCapital.

Link Cell Therapies' logic-gate technology builds CAR T cells that require co-expression of two
cancer targets before driving cell cytotoxicity, providing greater tumor specificity than existing
logic-gate technologies and aiming to avoid off-tumor toxicity. Lead disclosed program: LNK001, a
dual-targeting AND-gated CAR directed at clear cell renal cell carcinoma. In January 2026 the company
entered a strategic collaboration with Johnson & Johnson to develop logic-gate LINK CAR T therapies.

Headquarters: 220 E. Grand Avenue, South San Francisco, CA 94080
